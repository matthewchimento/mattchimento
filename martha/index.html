For Martha

100 Things I Love
About You

I could never actually stop at one hundred — but I had to stop somewhere, so here's a start. Every one of these is true, and there are a thousand more I didn't have room for.

  1. The way you listen to me like nothing else in the world matters more.

  2. How your love is so big and so present — nothing halfway about it.

  3. That you check on me even when you'd have every reason to be tired of me.

  4. The eerie number of ways we're just... the same person.

  5. How we notice the same things at the same time without saying a word.

  6. Your mind — genuinely one of the most brilliant I've ever come across.

  7. That you have a real, thought-out opinion on literally everything.

  8. How you can talk about a movie, a painting, and a book like you made all three.

  9. That you always know exactly what to say when I'm falling apart.

  10. How you make sad days smaller just by being in them.

  11. That you give gifts like it's your love language, thought out every time.

  12. Our "3... 2... 1..." before every single movie — never skipped, not once.

  13. Your accent. Every word sounds like it was made just for me to fall for.

  14. That everything I do now, I quietly measure against what you'd do.

  15. You're my blueprint, and I didn't even have to ask you to be.

  16. How we talk through entire movies and somehow it's my favorite part.

  17. That you scream every single time you get shot in Fortnite like it's personal.

  18. How you immediately, without fail, come revive me.

  19. That you ask me for help and actually trust me to give it.

  20. How emotionally mature you are — it makes me want to be better too.

  21. Everything you've survived, and you still choose to be soft.

  22. Your attitude toward life after everything life has thrown at you.

  23. Our shared hobby of gossiping about the people who copy us.

  24. Ice shaken espressos from Starbucks — our drink, our thing.

  25. That you listen to me even when I'm being insane and stupid.

  26. That I can hand you my trust and it's actually safe there.

  27. Your loyalty — it's not even a question with you.

  28. How patiently you helped me undo my trust issues.

  29. That you understand me in ways I still don't fully understand myself.

  30. Your running commentary during movies — genuinely the best part.

  31. How effortlessly funny you are, on purpose and by accident.

  32. That you can make me laugh on the worst days.

  33. Your face. I could just look at it.

  34. Your bone structure, unfairly perfect.

  35. Your body, and the confidence you carry it with.

  36. Your tan skin in the summer.

  37. Your hair — I love just touching it.

  38. Your voice when it goes soft, just for me.

  39. The Spanish you speak to your cats when you think no one's listening.

  40. How much you love animals, and how far you'll go for one in need.

  41. That you actually love yourself, and it shows.

  42. Your confidence — not loud, just certain.

  43. "Baby baby baby baby" every time you're under attack in Fortnite.

  44. Your energy, and everything it does to me.

  45. That you half-believe you're a witch, and I half-believe you too.

  46. Your astrology obsession — I know more birth charts now than I ever wanted to.

  47. That you remember everything, even things I forgot I said.

  48. How possessive you get over me — I'm not even a little upset about it.

  49. Your jealousy, which somehow just feels like proof.

  50. That you idealize me on days I can't stand myself.

  51. How you make me feel like I'm worth it, even insecure and messy.

  52. Your forgiveness, given more generously than I've earned it.

  53. How understanding you are when I don't deserve the benefit of the doubt.

  54. That you feel like home in a way I didn't think people could.

  55. How you make me feel chosen every single day.

  56. The way you say my name.

  57. That you remember the small things I mention once, in passing.

  58. How you turn ordinary nights into my favorite memories.

  59. The way you fight for me even when I'm not fighting for myself.

  60. That being loved by you feels like being understood completely.

  61. How you make being vulnerable with you feel safe instead of scary.

  62. That you never make me feel crazy for feeling things deeply.

  63. Your laugh — the real one, not the polite one.

  64. How you light up talking about something you actually love.

  65. That you never run out of things to say to me.

  66. The way our synchronicities keep piling up, like the universe is in on it.

  67. How similar we are, down to weirdly specific things.

  68. That you make me want to be a more thoughtful person.

  69. How you notice when I'm quiet and know exactly why.

  70. That you show up for me the way my grandma used to.

  71. How safe I feel telling you literally anything.

  72. That you never once made me feel like a burden.

  73. Your intelligence in an argument — terrifying and impressive.

  74. That you can hold your own opinion against anyone.

  75. How you explain art and literature like you're pulling back a curtain.

  76. That movies feel like a ritual with you, not just something to watch.

  77. How you make our inside jokes feel like their own language.

  78. That you care for your cats like they're your children.

  79. Your softness with anything smaller or weaker than you.

  80. That you notice when something's wrong before I say a word.

  81. How you handle my bad days without ever making them about you.

  82. That you still choose me on the days I'm hardest to choose.

  83. The way you get excited talking about the stars and what they mean.

  84. That you believe in things bigger than both of us, and let me believe too.

  85. How you make me feel like the most interesting person in the room.

  86. That you're endlessly curious about the world, and about me.

  87. Your taste — in movies, in books, in everything.

  88. That you never let a compliment just sit there — you always give one back.

  89. How you make plans just to spend more time with me.

  90. That you still get excited to see me.

  91. The way you fall asleep mid-conversation and I don't even mind.

  92. That you're the last person I want to talk to every single night.

  93. How our fights never feel like they threaten us.

  94. That you apologize first, even when you don't have to.

  95. How you make ordinary Tuesdays feel like something worth remembering.

  96. That being your person is the best thing I've ever been.

  97. The life I picture with you in it, without even trying to picture it.

  98. That you love me on my worst days as much as my best ones.

  99. How you make me believe I got impossibly lucky.

  100. That out of everyone, it's you — and I'd choose you again every time.

Martha — this is just a hundred. I ran out of room, not reasons. Thank you for loving me the way you do. I don't think I'll ever get used to how lucky that makes me.

— Matt
